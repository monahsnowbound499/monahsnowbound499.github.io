🛡️ How to Perform an Update

Updating your firmware requires care. Follow this procedure to ensure success:

  1. Open the Phoenix BIOS Tool using the shortcut on your desktop.
  2. Click the "Check for Updates" button to verify your current version status.
  3. Select "Backup" to save your current configuration to a secure location on your hard drive.
  4. After the backup finishes, verify the new firmware file location.
  5. Select "Flash" to start the writing process. Do not interact with the computer until the tool displays a success message.
  6. Restart your machine once prompted by the application.

🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the application shows an error, check these common fixes:

  • Access Denied: Ensure you are running the tool as an administrator. Right-click the icon and select "Run as administrator."
  • Update Fails: Check that your internet connection remains active during the verification step of the update.
  • Program Hangs: Close all other programs before launching the BIOS tool to ensure system stability.

⚠️ Safety Practices

Firmware modifications carry inherent risks. Never turn off your computer while the flash utility writes data to your motherboard. Interruptions during this phase can lead to system startup failures. Always verify that your laptop battery has at least 50% charge before beginning the process.
